Snow Leopard It is the rarest of the big cats, with an estimated 4,000 to 6,500 individuals left in the wild. Snow leopards are native to the mountain ranges of Central and South Asia, including the Himalayas. They are known for their beautiful spotted coats and their ability to survive in harsh, cold environments.
